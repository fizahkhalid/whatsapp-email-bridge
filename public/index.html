How it works

Three steps, then it runs quietly.

  1. 1

    Connect your accounts

    Link WhatsApp as a device and authorize the Gmail inbox used by the bridge.

  2. 2

    Choose conversations

    Select WhatsApp groups or add a direct chat using any international phone number.

  3. 3

    Use a simple prefix

    Only messages beginning with your chosen prefix are sent to email. Replies come back automatically.

Self-hosted

Your bridge runs where you choose.

Account credentials, selected conversations, and templates stay on your installation. The public page never displays them.
